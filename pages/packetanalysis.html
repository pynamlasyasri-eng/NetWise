Understanding Packet Analysis

Source IP: The address of the device sending the packet.

Destination IP: The address of the device receiving the packet.

Protocol: Defines the communication rules used by the packet.

Packet Size: The amount of data contained in a packet.

Port: Identifies the application or service endpoint.
