Star Network Topology

In star topology, all devices connect to a central switch. The switch connects them to the router and network.

⭐ Star Topology

Every device connects to a central hub or switch. It is easy to manage and troubleshoot.

🔗 Bus Topology

All devices share a single main communication cable. It is simple but can have a single point of failure.

🔄 Ring Topology

Devices connect in a circular arrangement. Data travels from one device to another around the ring.

🌐 Mesh Topology

Devices have multiple connections with other devices. It provides alternative paths for communication.
